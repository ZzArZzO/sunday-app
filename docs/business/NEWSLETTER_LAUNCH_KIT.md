# The Sunday Read — Newsletter Launch Kit

*Ready-to-paste copy for the Beehiiv publication `thesundayread.beehiiv.com`. Calm, publisher-side, MiFID-safe voice. Paste into Beehiiv; fill `[VERIFY: …]` placeholders with real, checked figures before sending.*

**Voice rules (non-negotiable):**
- Information & education only. **Never** "you should," "buy/sell," "we recommend," "take profits," "good time to."
- Describe and explain; don't direct. General/impersonal commentary = publisher exclusion (safe). Personalized advice = RIA/MiFID territory (not safe).
- Don't publish unverified numbers (YMYL accuracy). Every figure must be checkable.
- **Write like a human, not AI.** No em dashes or hyphens used as punctuation. No ellipses (`...`). Avoid AI tells: "it's not X, it's Y" framing, parallel triads, "a few things," over-tidy structure. Short, plain sentences.
- **Human-authored.** Afonso writes/edits these, so no "AI-generated" labelling is used. (Marking is only legally required for genuinely AI-generated content under the EU AI Act — if you ever auto-draft, mark it then.)
- Footer carries the disclaimer + Impressum + unsubscribe on every send.
